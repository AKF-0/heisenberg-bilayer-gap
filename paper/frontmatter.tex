\begin{abstract}
We give an explicit finite-certificate proof of a uniform spectral gap for the
antiferromagnetic spin-$\tfrac12$ square-lattice bilayer. With the rung coupling
normalized to one, every real intralayer coupling $0\le g\le1/10$ and every even
periodic square of side $L\ge256$ has a simple ground energy and spectral gap
strictly greater than $\log(200/13)/1024$. The finite initialization uses cubic
pair-creation trial states on $4\times4$, $8\times4$, and $8\times8$ rung tori.
Exact signed residual identities, finite occupation-space comparisons, and
rational polynomial certificates control the physical energy and partition
function bounds uniformly throughout the coupling interval. A real symmetric
spatial-transfer approximation supplies legitimate even-power moments without assuming
nonnegative transfer spectra. Three staggered purities, six ordered
unequal-error updates, and spatial interpolation then give the conclusion for
all sufficiently large even squares, not only a dyadic sequence. The argument
is formalized from the explicit Pauli Hamiltonian in Lean 4; its corrected
source snapshot has been rebuilt and audited. No assertion of a near-critical
parameter range, infinite-volume ground-state uniqueness, or global priority
is made.
\end{abstract}
