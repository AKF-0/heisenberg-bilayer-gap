\section{Verification, limitations, and reproducibility}\label{sec:verification}
The formal proof follows the mathematical dependencies above: explicit Pauli
operators, finite rung and occupation maps, local residual algebra, exact
finite coefficient certificates, physical seed ratios, and the all-size purity
criterion. Formalization is not a substitute for checking that these definitions
represent the intended model; the explicit basis maps and edge lists provide
that semantic connection. The companion \nolinkurl{docs/proof-map.md} records
specific theorem names and source locations. Machine-readable polynomial
arrays are appendices to the finite certificate, rather than a replacement
for the inequalities that justify their use.

\paragraph{Source-provenance correction.}
An earlier incremental snapshot did not establish source/object correspondence:
a later source edit was not represented by its previously built object, and the
first isolated rebuild exposed the discrepancy. That snapshot is not the
reproduction evidence for this manuscript. The source was corrected, a new
immutable input manifest was selected, and the entire project build and audit
were restarted successfully. The reproducibility documentation retains the
superseded receipt and the corrected terminal receipt as distinct records.

\paragraph{What remains outside the claim.}
The finite polynomial trial is non-asymptotic because every residual term is
retained and bounded on the entire interval. It is nevertheless organized
around the decoupled dimer limit. This does not establish a new general
nonperturbative phase theorem or a best-known coupling threshold. A broader
literature comparison and external mathematical review remain appropriate.
Further improvements could sharpen the finite certificate or the bootstrap,
but are not needed for the theorem stated here. Reproducing the present result
requires the pinned finite source checks, not an HPC spectrum calculation.
