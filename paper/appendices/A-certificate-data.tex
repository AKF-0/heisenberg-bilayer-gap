The actual kernel check gives both $qI-\widetilde K^2\ge0$ and its positive
absolute-kernel counterpart, with trace bounds $T_A$. The endpoint covariance
constants are \eqref{eq:rowSquares} inserted into $\tau_A$, not estimates of
unknown infinite-volume correlations.

The pair coefficient can also be generated by a compact matrix expression.
Let $Z_0=\diag((DK)_{rr})$ and use $\circ$ for entrywise multiplication. The
symmetric coefficient $F$ in Section~\ref{sec:moments} is exactly
\begin{equation}\label{eq:compactpair}
 F=2K+\frac g2D+\frac g2\off(DK+KD)-g(D\circ K)
 +\frac g2\off(KDK)-2g(Z_0K+KZ_0)+2g(D\circ K\circ K).
\end{equation}
The finite regrouping from the literal edge polynomial \eqref{eq:q2} to
\eqref{eq:compactpair} is proved separately. It is not substituted as an ansatz.
For translation-invariant $K$, its rooted row then gives the exact pair vacuum
norm and mean coefficient used by the table.

The quartic mean integer aggregate is, explicitly,
\[
 \frac A4\sum_{\substack{S\ni0\\|S|=4}}\sum_{m=0}^2
       u_{S,m}\left(6p_{S,m}+3\sum_{m'=0}^2p_{S,m'}\right),
\]
where $u_{S,m}$ is the proved whole-interval envelope of the \emph{signed}
canonical coefficient and every $p$ uses the exact endpoint covariance.
In the integer implementation the denominator is the envelope denominator
multiplied by the square of the covariance denominator. The normalization
$524288$ has already been included in the coefficient envelope and is not
applied a second time. The sextic aggregate is bounded by
$405\ell_{A,6}(\max L)^3$. The final physical mean is the sum of the pair,
quartic and sextic aggregates times $M_{A,0}$.
